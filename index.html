Deep Tide

A small ocean that runs without you. How much of it do you take?

What you're hearing
  • The chord is the ocean. Every species is one note, as loud as that species is common. When a species dies out, its note goes silent.
  • A note that clashes means invaders are here.
  • Muffled sound is polluted water, or a dead zone with no oxygen left. A wavering chord is water getting too warm. Night sounds darker.
  • Bells ring often in an ocean with many species, and fall quiet as it loses them.
  • Engine: a trawler at work. Splash: a net haul. Horn: a ship flushing ballast. Gong: a species just died out.
  • Clicks: a sperm whale hunting in the deep. Everything sounds darker the deeper you look.

The Sound button, or M, switches it off and on.
